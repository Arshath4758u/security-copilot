# Sample security policy (prototype)

## Password policy
Passwords must be at least 12 characters, changed every 90 days, and never reused across the last 5 passwords. Multi-factor authentication is required for all administrator accounts.

## Data breach response
Report a suspected breach to the security team within 1 hour. The security team assesses the scope within 24 hours. Customer notification follows legal guidance.

## Access control
Access follows least privilege. Administrator access needs a ticket and manager approval, and is reviewed every quarter.
